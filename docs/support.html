PANNA — Support

Found a bug, got stuck, or have an idea? Open an issue and tell us what happened (your device and what you were doing helps a lot).

Quick answers

No sound? Turn your volume up — PANNA plays even with the silent switch on.
Playing a friend: Online → Create Room → Invite. Your friend taps the link or types the 4-letter code.
Lost connection mid-match? Reopen the app within 90 seconds and you'll rejoin your seat.
