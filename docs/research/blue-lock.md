# Blue Lock: why it works, and what PANNA should take from it

Research date 2026-09-29. Nothing below copies names or characters. Every idea is a mechanic or a tone choice.

---

The poll and fan-art pattern tells us the **character roster is the product**, not the football.

## 2. Why it broke out

1. **It flips the sports-shōnen premise.** Teamwork-and-friendship is replaced by *ego*. The thesis is that a striker must be selfish to win. Critics single this out as the reason people who don't watch sports anime watch it ([CBR](https://www.cbr.com/why-bluelock-is-more-than-a-sports-anime/), [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/blue-lock-battle-royale-sports-anime/)).
2. **A death-game structure with sports stakes.** 300 strikers go in and one comes out. Losers are banned from ever playing for Japan. IGN called it *Squid Game* with a ball ([Wikipedia reception](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blue_Lock)). Each match is an elimination, so no match is filler.
3. **Everything is a visible number.** Every player has a rank (#1–#300), and later a yen valuation. Readers track them like a leaderboard.
4. **Players are defined by one "weapon".** Each character has a single legible signature: a left foot, a dribble, a spin, spatial reading, a first touch. That makes 30+ characters memorable at a glance.
5. **Growth is shown as an "awakening".** Eyes glow, the art style breaks, and an inner monologue takes over.
6. **Rivals get absorbed.** The lead wins by *devouring* opponents' weapons (see §3). Every rival feeds the protagonist.
7. **Real-world timing.** The anime launched the same month Japan's World Cup run happened, and the series' creators were attached to Japan's 2022 kit campaign.

Warning: S2 was mocked as a "slideshow" with still frames during awakenings ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/blue-lock-season-2-controversy-bad-animation-martinkiings/), [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/blue-lock-season-2-what-happened-to-the-animation/)). Fans notice cut corners exactly on the hype beats.

## 3. The gamified systems inside the fiction

| Blue Lock concept | What it is | Game-design reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Ranking #1–#300** | A public, individual rank that updates after every stage | A persistent visible ladder number. Identity = the number |
| **1st Selection** | Teams of 11 in round-robin groups. Top scorers advance even when their team loses | Individual score beats team result. A "carry your way out" rule |
| **2nd Selection** | Win a 3-player team match, then *take* one player from the losing team. Losers split up and re-form | A draft-steal mechanic. Winners grow their squad, losers get recycled |
| **3rd Selection / U-20 match** | The best form a squad against the establishment | A tentpole "boss" event after the grind |
| **Neo Egoist League (NEL)** | 5 European-club-style teams play a round robin. Real clubs **bid on players in yen after every match**. Top 23 bids make the national squad | A market value that moves every match. Bids ranged ¥5M → ¥400M. A goal was worth roughly +¥30–50M, an assist about +¥20M, and invisible games meant stagnation or a drop ([NEL wiki](https://bluelock.fandom.com/wiki/Neo_Egoist_League), [Blue Lock Archive](https://bluelockarchive.com/selections/neo-egoist-league)) |
| **Weapon** | One signature skill per player | Loadout identity. A single passive/active that defines the build |
| **Flow** | A trance where the body acts before thought. Everything clicks for a stretch of play ([Isagi wiki](https://bluelock.fandom.com/wiki/Yoichi_Isagi)) | Timed super mode (PANNA already has this: Hype → 8 s Flow) |
| **Metavision** | Seeing the whole pitch from above and predicting where everyone will be ([Sportskeeda](https://www.sportskeeda.com/anime/isagi-s-arsenal-blue-lock-metavision-clearly-shows-growth-skills)) | A readable UI power: pass lanes and runs shown ahead of time |
| **Devouring** | Watching a rival, turning their weapon into data, then using it against them | Learn-from-opponent progression |
| **Chemical reaction** | Two egos that happen to click and produce a combo neither could alone | Pair synergies between specific player types |

The core trick: **football's rules never change**. Only the *scoring of people* does, through ranks, bids and eliminations. The fiction lives in the meta layer and in presentation, not the physics. That is the model for PANNA.

## 4. Existing Blue Lock games

| Game | Type | Result | Lessons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Project: World Champion** (Rudel Inc.; JP 2022, global Apr 2024. *Not* Rush/Bandai) | Coach sim. Train strikers, auto-played matches, gacha characters and support cards | #1 App Store JP in H1 2023, Sensor Tower APAC "Best Sports Manager Game" 2023 ([Pocket Gamer](https://www.pocketgamer.com/blue-lock-project-world-champion/out-now/)), **17M downloads by its 3rd anniversary** ([BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/news/blue-lock-project-world-champion-japan-release-announcement-en.html)), 4.7★ iOS | Liked: generous start, a **free 11-pull on every new banner**, original training story. Hated: two stamina timers (training 1 per 10 min; matches 1 per 100 min, cap 5), repeated dialogue, sudden difficulty spikes ([Gacha wiki](https://gachagames.fandom.com/wiki/BLUE_LOCK_Project:_World_Champion)). Uma-Musume-style training monetises well without real-time play |
| **Blaze Battle** (BAEL; Mar 2024) | 3D 11v11 action, character gacha | **Shut down 31 Mar 2026** after about 2 years ([ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-03-02/blue-lock-blaze-battle-malevolent-spirits-mr-love-queen-choice-smartphone-games-to-end-service/.234796)) | Cutscenes and anime callbacks were "the biggest highlight". Server errors and performance problems hurt it. JP-only. The press called the IP "left without a solid game" ([GamingonPhone](https://gamingonphone.com/news/blue-lock-blaze-battle-to-shut-down-this-march-2026-another-big-anime-ip-left-without-a-solid-game-is-a-head-scratcher)). **11v11 real-time on mobile plus shaky netcode = death** |
| **eFootball 2024 collab** | Cosmetic crossover | Short-lived | Brand halo only |
| **Blue Lock: Rivals** (Roblox, fan-made; Jul 2024) | **5v5, you control ONE player, 6–8 min matches**, an Ego meter filled by tackles/passes/goals that unlocks a signature ability, gacha "style" and "flow" spins, ranked | **4.4–4.95B visits, roughly 15–33k live players** ([Rolimon's](https://www.rolimons.com/game/18668065416), [ServicesPV](https://servicespv.com/en/roblox/stats/raichi-blue-lock-rivals-by-blue-lock-rivals-unofficial-fans-roblox), [Earnaldo](https://earnaldo.com/blog/blue-lock-rivals-vs-volleyball-legends)) | **This is the closest comparable to PANNA, and it is the only big success.** Small-sided play, one avatar, a meter into a signature power, and a gacha for playstyles. Its known weakness is front-loaded progression that stalls once free spins run out |

**Takeaway.** The official games copied the *roster* (training sim) or the *sport* (11v11). The fan game that copied the *fantasy*, "I am one egoist with a weapon, in a small game, with a meter that makes me explode", is the one that took off. PANNA's premise already sits in that gap, and there is no strong global Blue Lock-like on iOS. The risk is the comparison with Rivals, so PANNA has to win on craft: controls, readable 3v3, anime-quality Flow.

## 5. Twenty ideas for PANNA

Names are placeholders chosen to avoid Blue Lock terms.

### Identity & progression
1. **Market Value (MV) as the headline number.** It shows on the profile, in the lobby and on the loading card. It starts at ¥/€/"◆" 1M and uses log-scaled gains. Per match: goal +3–5%, assist +2%, panna +1%, win +3%, loss −1%, AFK/abandon −5%. Clamp to ±12% per match so one blowout can't dominate. Show a stock-ticker arrow after every match. Keep MV (performance, can go down) separate from Rank (competitive tier) so casual play still moves a number.
2. **A public rank number, not just a tier.** "#48,210 → #47,655" beats "Gold II". Show the global number, the country number and the crew number. Add a weekly "Top 300" board. **300 is a hard cap** (the Blue Lock intake size) and gets its own badge.
3. **One Signature Weapon per footballer.** At creation, pick 1 of 6 archetypes (e.g. Cannon, Ghost Dribble, Aerial, Radar, Wall, Trickster). The weapon is a *passive* that's always on (+15% in its niche) and gives the Flow ability its look. Legacy cards modify the weapon but never replace it, so players stay "the X guy".
4. **Awakening tiers on the weapon.** Three awakenings per weapon, unlocked by *doing the thing* (e.g. 50 nutmegs, 20 volley goals), not by paying. Each adds a visual layer (eye glint → aura trail → full-screen colour break). Awakenings are the long-term goal. Gacha only sells Prospects and cosmetics.
5. **Devour (learn from rivals).** When an opponent scores on you with a Legacy technique, you gain "Study" points for that technique (e.g. 1 kill-cam = 10%). At 100% you unlock a *bronze copy* you can equip until the season ends. This is a free, skill-based alternative to pulling. Cap it at 1 devoured technique equipped, so the gacha still matters.
6. **Nemesis tracking.** The game remembers the player who beat you most this season and pings you: "Your nemesis is online, ranked #3,102. Queue into them?" Beating them grants a one-time MV bonus (+8%) and a "Devoured" stamp on your profile. Also run a rivalry feed inside crews.

### Match moments (3-minute, 3v3)
7. **Hype sources that reward ego *and* football.** Keep the meter, but weight the inputs: panna +25, skill move that beats a defender +15, shot on target +10, key pass +10, tackle +10, goal +40. Passive decay starts after 6 s idle. Aim for **1–2 Flows per player per match**. Tune so the first Flow lands around the 60–80 s mark.
8. **Flow = colour break, not just stats.** On trigger: 0.4 s freeze-frame, the eyes flare in the weapon's colour, the camera punches in, the crowd audio drops to heartbeat and breath, and the world desaturates except for your trail. Stats during the 8 s: +20% speed, +25% shot power, perfect first touch, and the weapon passive doubles. **Never lock the player's controls for the cut-in**, keep it at 0.4 s max.
9. **A Radar sense inside Flow.** For the Radar weapon (and as a mild universal Flow perk), ghost lines show teammates' and opponents' positions 0.5 s into the future.
10. **Inner-monologue barks.** Short text lines slam in over the HUD on Flow, first goal, nemesis goal and panna. They are written per weapon: "Mine." / "I see all of it." / "Too slow." VO only on Flow. Max 1 bark per 20 s.
11. **Pair Reaction (chemistry).** Specific weapon pairs (e.g. Radar + Cannon, Trickster + Ghost Dribble) do a combo when both are in Flow within 3 s of each other. The result is a synced one-two with a split-screen cut-in. This rewards co-op and crews without breaking the ego fantasy.
12. **Ego vs. assist dilemma.** Show a tiny "shot xG vs. pass xG" flash when you're in range (as an option, off in ranked). Scoring yourself gives more MV. Assisting gives more Hype to both players. The selfish/team tension becomes a real decision.
13. **Man of the Match = "Last Egoist Standing".** After the match there's one crowned player out of all 6. It's the biggest MV bump (+5%) and gets a spotlight pose screen.

### Career & live structure
14. **Selection-style career arcs.** Chapter 1 "The Facility": a 64-player *simulated* ladder where you must finish in the top X to advance (an elimination, with a replay allowed). Chapter 2 "The Steal": beat a Prospect-led team to recruit one of theirs (maps onto Prospect collection). Chapter 3 "The Establishment": boss matches vs. an elite youth XI. Chapter 4 "The League": clubs bid on you (see 15).
15. **The Bid Board (season finale).** In the last week of each season, sponsor "clubs" (original fictional brands) make offers based on MV and highlight stats. The top 1% get a "Signed" cosmetic kit and a leaderboard crest. Everyone gets *some* bid, the floor is never zero, so it's motivating rather than humiliating.
16. **Eliminations only where they're fun.** Weekend "Lock-In" event: 3 lives, and 3 losses take you out. Your run rank is the reward tier. This is where the death-game stakes live. **Don't** put permanent loss or demotion walls in normal ranked.
17. **Prospects have a weapon, a rank and a hook line.** Every gacha Prospect has a one-line ego motto, a weapon, a rival Prospect, and an awakening you can see (and show off). The roster is the fandom engine. Poll the community on Prospect popularity each season, since Blue Lock's polls themselves drive engagement.

### Monetization & tone
18. **Copy what worked in PWC.** A free 10-pull on every new banner. No double stamina. At most 1 soft energy, or none for PvP. Unlock the Scout Pack pity on-screen. Avoid PWC's hated 100-minute match timer.
19. **Awakening cosmetics are the premium shelf.** Eye colours, aura trails, Flow cut-in frames and bark voice packs. These are pure fantasy with zero stat power, which keeps ranked clean.
20. **Tone bible.** Every screen should feel like being judged: numbers everywhere, harsh-but-cool copy ("You were invisible today. −1%"), a stark lockdown-facility palette in menus (black / electric blue / warning yellow) against the colourful cel-shaded pitch. Coach-voice notifications. Avoid the words *Blue Lock, Ego, Egoist, Metavision, Flow chemistry, devour* in UI (Flow is generic enough to keep). Use *Weapon/Signature, Radar, Study/Consume, Reaction*.

## 6. What will not work in a 3-minute mobile match

- **Long awakening cutscenes.** The anime spends pages on one moment. In PvP, anything over about 0.5 s of lost control feels like being robbed. Put the long version in career cutscenes and replays/share clips, not live play.
- **Literal elimination.** Losing access permanently is great fiction and bad retention. Use events (16) and seasonal bids (15) instead.
- **11-player tactics and real Metavision.** 3v3 has no midfield structure to "read". Reduce it to short ghost lines (9).
- **"Steal a player from the loser" in PvP.** It punishes losers with loss of an owned asset, which is toxic in F2P. Keep it only for career (14) and for devoured *bronze copies* (5).
- **Pure selfishness.** Blue Lock's theme punishes passing. In 3v3 online, ball-hogs ruin matches for teammates. Idea 12 keeps passing worth it through Hype.
- **Too many numbers mid-match.** MV and rank belong on the post-match screen. During play, show only Hype, the score and the clock.
- **Official-IP-style 11v11 plus weak netcode.** That is why Blaze Battle died. PANNA's 3v3 is the right call. Protect frame rate and rollback/latency before adding spectacle.

## 7. First cut
Ideas 1, 2, 3, 7, 8, 10, 13 deliver most of the feeling for the least engineering. The rest come later.
