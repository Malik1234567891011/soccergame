PANNA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

PANNA is a street-football game. We collect as little as possible: only what the game needs to work.

What stays on your device

Your profile (name, look, unlocks, progress, settings) is saved on your iPhone. Gameplay statistics used to tune the game are also stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

Online play

When you open the Online screen, the game connects to the PANNA game server and sends a random player ID created on your device, the display name you chose, your footballer's look and loadout, and your rank points. The server keeps that (player ID, name, rank, crew membership) so leaderboards, crews and private rooms work. Match inputs are sent while you play and are not stored.

What we don't do

No ads. No third-party analytics or tracking. No account, email, contacts, location or photos are collected. We don't sell or share data with anyone.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

PANNA doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children. Display names are filtered for offensive words.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. To remove your online player record, open an issue on our support page with your display name.
